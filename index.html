Privacy Policy

Taipei Layover
Last updated: September 30, 2026

Taipei Layover is designed to provide practical travel information for airline crew and short-stay travelers visiting Taipei.

Information We Collect

Taipei Layover does not require you to create an account or sign in. The app does not collect personal information for its core travel guide features.

Taipei Layover does not use personal information for advertising, marketing, or the creation of user profiles.

Saved Places and Plans

Places you save and itinerary information you add to My Plan are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to us.

Contacting Us

If you choose to contact us by email, we will receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

This information is used only to respond to your question, feedback, or support request. We do not use this information for advertising or marketing.

Third-Party Services and Links

Taipei Layover may provide links that open third-party websites or apps, such as mapping, transportation, booking, shopping, or other travel-related services.

When you choose to use a third-party service, that service operates according to its own terms and privacy practices. Taipei Layover does not control how third-party services collect or process information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information that you voluntarily send to us by email may be retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry or maintain appropriate correspondence records.

You may request deletion of information you have provided to us by contacting us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Taipei Layover does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
